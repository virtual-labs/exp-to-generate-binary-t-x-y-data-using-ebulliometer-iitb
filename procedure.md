

<h3>Purge the System with Nitrogen gas to remove contaminants.</h3>
<ol type='1'>
    <li>Open the outlet of Raal’s apparatus. </li>
    <li>Start Nitrogen Gas. </li>
    <li>Allow gas to flow through the system for few minutes. </li>
    <li>Shut off nitrogen supply after pop up massage for purging completion is displayed. </li>
</ol>

<h3>Generating boiling point data at Atmospheric pressure <h3>
<ol type='1'>
    <li> Select the system and composition from the dropdown list. </li>
    <li> Close the outlet of Raal’s apparatus and open the inlet. </li>
   <li> Close the inlet of Raal’s apparatus after it is filled. </li>
    <li> Start cold water pump. </li>
    <li> Start Heating coil. </li>
    <li> Increase heating rate slowly by moving the sliding bar. </li>
    <li> Allow system to accommodate change in the heat. </li>
    <li> Observe the change in the temperature. </li>
    <li> Do the same until small increment in heating rate won’t rising temperature. </li>
    <li> Take the liquid and vapor samples. </li>
    <li> Repeat the procedure for different compositions.  </li>
</ol>

<h3>Sample Analysis. </h3>
<ol type='1'>
     <li>  Make 4 samples containing known mass of individual components of system + external standard (ES).  </li> 
     <li>  Calibrate GC with this sample and note down the area for each. </li> 
     <li>  Plot the graph of mass ratio of component to ES Vs area ratio of component to ES. </li> 
     <li>  Calculate slope. </li> 
     <li>  Analyze samples using GC. </li> 
     <li>  Use slope and basic information of individual compounds (i.e. molecular wt., Density,…) to calculate mole fraction. </li> 

</ol>
